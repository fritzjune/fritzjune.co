Fair questions.

So, are you a VA or not?

I do plenty of hands-on work. The difference is I'm working from your goals, not a task list. I'll tell you when something doesn't make sense, and I'll fix the system so the same problem doesn't come back.

Who do you usually work with?

Established founders and small leadership teams who already have traction, a product people buy and a team in motion, and need someone senior enough to keep it all joined up.

Do you replace my marketing team or ops manager?

No. I make them more effective. I sit between the functions, keep priorities clear and make sure the handoffs land.

What tools do you work in?

Whatever you already use. Notion, ClickUp, Asana, HubSpot, Google Workspace, Slack, your email platform of choice. If something's slowing you down, I'll say so and suggest a better setup.

What happens on the call?

We talk about your business and where it's stuck. If I'm the right fit, I'll tell you how I'd help. If I'm not, I'll tell you that too.
